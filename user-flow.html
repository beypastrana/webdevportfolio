Why this flow

Buyers are asked to log in only right before checkout, not before browsing — letting them explore the marketplace freely first builds trust before asking for any commitment, which matters for a first-time user on a new app.

Edge cases covered

Out-of-stock items redirect to similar listings instead of a dead end, and failed payments loop back to retry rather than losing the cart — both reduce drop-off at the most fragile step of the journey.
