UX Case Study · FarmMarket PH · 02 / 06

Customer Journey Map

Following Maria, a small-scale vegetable farmer, from the moment she hears about FarmMarket PH to becoming a regular seller — mapping her actions, thoughts, emotions, and the pain points that shaped the product's design decisions.

M

Maria Santos — Farmer, 42, Nueva Ecija

Grows vegetables on a 1.5-hectare farm. Relies on a middleman (viajero) who takes a large cut of her earnings. Owns an Android phone, uses Facebook and GCash daily.

Goal: sell produce directly to buyers for a fairer price, without needing a computer or complex tools

Emotional curve across the journey

Awareness Consideration Onboarding First Sale Advocacy
Actions Sees FarmMarket PH mentioned in a farmers' Facebook group Asks a neighbor who already uses it; watches a demo video Downloads the app, signs up, sets up her farm profile Lists her first batch of tomatoes, receives an order Posts about her results in the same Facebook group
Touchpoints Facebook post, word of mouth Neighbor's phone, YouTube demo, Play Store listing Sign-up flow, role selection, listing form Push notification, in-app chat, GCash payment Facebook, in-app referral link
Thoughts "Is this real? Will it actually pay better than the viajero?" "Looks simple enough, but I'm not techy — will I get lost?" "So many fields... do I need photos of everything?" "Someone actually wants to buy directly from me!" "My neighbors should try this too."
Emotions Curious, a little skeptical Cautiously hopeful Slightly overwhelmed Excited, validated Proud, confident
Pain Points Distrust of new apps promising better income No one nearby to walk her through setup in person Listing form feels long on a small screen; unsure about pricing Anxious waiting for buyer messages with no clear status No easy way to invite others and track who joined
Opportunities Show real farmer earnings/testimonials early in marketing In-app video walkthrough in Tagalog, no account needed to preview Break listing into 3 short steps with a suggested price guide Add order status badges (New · Packed · Picked up) Simple "Invite a farmer" link with a shared rewards incentive
