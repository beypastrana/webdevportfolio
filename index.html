About Me

I sit at the intersection of design and development — I design interfaces in Figma and Adobe XD, then build them out as real, responsive websites and mobile app screens.

My process starts with the user: how they think, what they need to find, and how few steps it should take them to get there. Then I bring that structure to life with clean code.

Whether it's a landing page, a full web app, or a mobile app UI, I care about the same thing — does it feel effortless to use?
