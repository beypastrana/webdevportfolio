Visual direction

Warm produce photography colors (leaf green, tomato red) sit on top of the brand's pink-and-black shell — keeping FarmMarket PH recognizably part of the same design system as the rest of this portfolio, while feeling distinct as its own product.

From wireframe to mockup

Every structural decision from the wireframe stage carried over unchanged — the mockup phase only added color, typography, real copy, and iconography. This kept the design review focused on visuals, since layout was already validated.
