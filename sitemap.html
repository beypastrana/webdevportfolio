Why this structure

FarmMarket PH serves two very different users in one app — farmers who need a fast way to list produce, and buyers who need to browse and order easily. Splitting into two clear top-level sections after role selection keeps each experience focused instead of forcing one generic menu on both.

Key decisions

  • Role is chosen once at onboarding, not re-asked every session
  • Messages and Notifications are shared so farmers and buyers can talk directly, no middleman
  • Add/Edit Listing sits one tap from the Farmer Dashboard — the core seller action
  • Cart and Checkout are merged into a single flow to reduce steps for buyers on mobile data
